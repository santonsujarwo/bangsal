## Homes shaped around the way you live

A residential project begins with understanding the people who will use the space. This sample service covers the typical stages involved in planning a new home, extension, or major residential improvement.

### A considered project sequence

1. **Brief and site review** — outline the goals, site context, approximate area, and key constraints.
2. **Scope and coordination** — align design information, material expectations, responsibilities, and milestones.
3. **Construction phase** — coordinate site activities and keep decisions visible as work progresses.
4. **Finishing and handover** — review completed work, record outstanding items, and prepare handover information.

### Suitable project types

- New residential builds
- Home extensions and additions
- Structural and layout improvements
- Selected refurbishment projects

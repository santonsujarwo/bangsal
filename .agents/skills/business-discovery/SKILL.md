# Business Discovery
1. Review supplied business material and existing website.
2. Separate verified facts, hypotheses, and unanswered questions.
3. Identify audiences, user intent, offer priorities, geography, proof points, and conversion actions.
4. Never invent credentials, testimonials, statistics, or claims.
5. Produce a brief using templates/business-brief.md.
6. Ask only for information that materially blocks the next phase.
